# Contributing to InclusionMed

[Project overview](README.md) · [Homepage](https://inclusionmed.ai/) · [Online form](https://inclusionmed.ai/#/contribute)

## Contribute to InclusionMed.

Share your work, ideas or expertise through the online form. Choose the type that fits what you have in mind:

| Type                       | What to describe                                                               | Optional link           |
|:-------------------------- |:------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |:----------------------- |
| **Benchmark**              | Your benchmark, dataset or evaluation tool, and what it evaluates              | Benchmark or project    |
| **Benchmark Idea**         | Your idea and the healthcare need it would address                             | Supporting reference    |
| **Correction**             | The benchmark or page, what should change and why                              | Supporting source       |
| **Advisory & Partnership** | Your expertise or organization’s focus, and how you would like to work with us | Profile or organization |

## In two steps.

![Two steps: share your contribution through the online form, then develop it together with the organizers by email.](assets/figures/contribution-workflow.svg)

### 1. Share your contribution

Share the work, insight or collaboration you’d like to bring to InclusionMed. We’ll review your message and continue the conversation by email.

Open the **[online form](https://inclusionmed.ai/#/contribute)** under **Share your contribution**, choose a contribution type, and enter your name and email. Add a title and a short description. Your organization and a public reference link are optional. A brief outline is enough to start.

Select **Review message**, check your details, then select **Submit**. Once saved, the page shows **Your message has been received.** and a reference number. Keep the reference for follow-up.

If submission fails, use **Retry submission**. You can also use **Copy message** and email it to [info@inclusionmed.ai](mailto:info@inclusionmed.ai). Use public links and leave out patient information, credentials and restricted source data.

The organizers review your message and confirm the next step by email. For benchmarks and ideas, this includes an initial review of relevance, evidence and feasibility against our [selection criteria](https://inclusionmed.ai/#/methodology), listed under **Criteria** on the Method page. We’ll explain any missing information or reasons the work cannot proceed.

### 2. Develop it together

We’ll follow up by email to explore your contribution and agree on what comes next. Before publication or announcement, we’ll **confirm the outcome** with you, including how your involvement is credited.

- **Benchmark:** We’ll agree on the materials needed and work with you through review, reproduction and any revisions. Once approved, we’ll **include and release** the benchmark with its scope, limitations and contributor credits.
- **Benchmark Idea:** We’ll explore the healthcare need behind your idea and shape a first task together. If it develops into a benchmark, we’ll support the review and **include and release** the accepted work with contributor credits.
- **Correction:** We’ll review the entry and your supporting evidence, then discuss any details with you. Once verified, we’ll **update and publish** the correction with its sources and contributor credits.
- **Advisory & Partnership:** We’ll explore shared interests and a role that suits your expertise or organization. Together, we’ll **confirm the collaboration** once we’ve agreed on scope, availability and how your involvement will be recognized.

For benchmark materials, see **[Data and code baseline v0.1](assets/docs/task-format.md)** for formats, examples and code conventions. We’ll agree on the materials needed as the work develops.

## Review timing

The organizers aim to:

| Milestone            | Target                                                                              |
|:-------------------- |:----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| First reply by email | Within **5 working days** of submission                                             |
| Initial review       | Within **15 working days** after the information needed for that review is complete |

These are review targets. Detailed review, reproduction, revisions and publication follow a schedule agreed by email. If additional information, access approvals or longer evaluations affect the schedule, we’ll confirm an updated date with you.
